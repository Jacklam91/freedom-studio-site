# Privacy Policy — Freedom Studio
Freedom Studio is a private tool used by a single owner to upload videos to, and read analytics from, the owner's own YouTube channels.
- It accesses only the owner's own YouTube account data, via Google OAuth, on the owner's own computer.
- It does not collect, store, sell or share any other person's data. Tokens are stored locally and never transmitted to third parties.
